## Page 1: 안내 및 동의

**양식 제목:** AI Research Hypothesis Evaluation Study

**양식 설명 (그대로 복사):**
```
Thank you for participating in this study. You will evaluate 12 AI research hypotheses
generated by an automated system that analyzes pre-existing AI literature.

Your task:
- Read each hypothesis carefully
- Rate it on 4 dimensions (1-10 scale)
- Provide an optional comment

Please:
- Evaluate independently (do not research the hypothesis first)
- Use your honest intuition as an AI researcher
- Complete all 12 hypotheses (20-30 minutes total)

Rating dimensions:
- Novelty (1-10): How genuinely new is this idea? (1 = well-known, 10 = completely novel)
- Feasibility (1-10): Could this be tested within 1-2 years? (1 = impossible, 10 = straightforward)
- Impact (1-10): If validated, how significant? (1 = minor, 10 = field-changing)
- Clarity (1-10): Is the hypothesis precise enough to test? (1 = too vague, 10 = immediately testable)

All responses are anonymous. Results will be used in a research paper (NeurIPS 2026).
Evaluators will be acknowledged in the paper.
```

### Page 7 — H05

**섹션 설명:**
```
Paradigm area: Attention Mechanisms

Original assumption being challenged:
"Self-attention over all token pairs is necessary for capturing long-range dependencies"

Research hypothesis:
"Sparse attention patterns that only attend to nearby tokens and a small number of global tokens can capture all necessary long-range dependencies at much lower cost"

Rationale:
Full attention may be computationally wasteful. Sparse patterns like those in Longformer suggest that selective attention might be sufficient for most dependencies.
```

### Page 8 — H06

**섹션 설명:**
```
Paradigm area: Autoregressive LLMs

Original assumption being challenged:
"Next-token prediction is the optimal training objective for language understanding"

Research hypothesis:
"Training language models to predict full sentence completions or paragraph-level summaries rather than individual tokens could lead to better language understanding"

Rationale:
Token-level prediction may focus models too narrowly on local context. Higher-level prediction tasks might encourage the development of broader semantic understanding.
```

### Page 9 — H07

**섹션 설명:**
```
Paradigm area: Autoregressive LLMs

Original assumption being challenged:
"Autoregressive generation is necessary for coherent long-form text"

Research hypothesis:
"Non-autoregressive models that generate all tokens in parallel with iterative refinement can produce more coherent long-form text than left-to-right generation"

Rationale:
Autoregressive generation forces local decisions without global planning. Parallel generation with multiple refinement passes could allow more globally coherent document planning.
```

## Google Forms 설정

1. Settings → "Collect email addresses" = **OFF** (익명)
2. Settings → "Limit to 1 response" = OFF (같은 사람이 수정 가능하도록)
3. Presentation → "Show progress bar" = **ON**
4. Presentation → "Shuffle question order" = OFF (가설 순서 고정)
5. 모든 평가 질문(Novelty/Feasibility/Impact/Clarity)은 **필수**로 설정
6. Optional comment는 **필수 아님**으로 설정

## 공유 방법

1. "Send" 버튼 → 링크 복사
2. "Shorten URL" 체크
3. 모집 이메일/DM에 링크 포함

## 응답 분석

1. Responses 탭 → Google Sheets 연동
2. CSV로 내보내기
3. `scripts/analyze_human_eval.py`로 분석 (추후 작성)
