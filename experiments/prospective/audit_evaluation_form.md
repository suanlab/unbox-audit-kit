## Section 0: Instructions

**Title:** AI Research Hypothesis Evaluation Study

**Description:**
Thank you for participating in this study. You will evaluate 12 AI research hypotheses generated by an automated system that analyzes pre-existing AI literature.

**Your task:**
- Read each hypothesis carefully
- Rate it on 4 dimensions (1–10 scale)
- Provide an optional comment

**Please:**
- Evaluate independently (do not research the hypothesis first)
- Use your honest intuition as an AI researcher
- Complete all 12 hypotheses (20–30 minutes total)

**Rating dimensions:**
- **Novelty (1-10):** How genuinely new is this idea? (1 = well-known or trivial, 10 = completely novel to you)
- **Feasibility (1-10):** Could this be tested within 1-2 years by a small team? (1 = impossible with current resources, 10 = straightforward experiment)
- **Impact (1-10):** If validated, how significant would the contribution be? (1 = minor improvement, 10 = field-changing paradigm shift)
- **Clarity (1-10):** Is the hypothesis precise enough to design experiments? (1 = too vague, 10 = immediately testable)
